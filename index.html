The Token

The $CITY Token

The $CITY token is the first gateway into our ecosystem, granting utility access, revenue sharing, and collective governance.

Total Supply

1,000,000,000 total starting supply. Locked liquidity pool. LP fees are split 50/50 between holder rewards and immediate buybacks or burns.

Fair Distribution

$CITY was created via a fair, public launch. Developer supply is reserved for project development and to benefit all holders of $CITY.

Benefits

$CITY powers participation across Alpha City. Free tools remain open to everyone, while combined liquid and staked holdings of 1,000,000 $CITY unlock the premium utility catalog.
