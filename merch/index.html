Before you order

Everything you need to know about the first Alpha City merchandise drop.

When will Drop 001 be available?+

We are finalizing products and designs now. This page will switch from preview mode as soon as product links are published.

Do I need a Sui wallet to buy merch?+

No. The shared wallet button keeps your Alpha City session available, but merchandise checkout uses standard payment methods and does not require a blockchain transaction.

Who makes and ships the products?+

Fourthwall coordinates on-demand production, payment, shipping, and order support for products in the Alpha City collection.

Will orders ship internationally?+

Fourthwall works with fulfillment partners in multiple regions and supports worldwide shipping for eligible products. Available methods and final shipping costs appear at checkout.

How do returns or damaged items work?+

Order questions are handled through the support link in your Fourthwall order confirmation. Product-specific return details will also appear before checkout.
